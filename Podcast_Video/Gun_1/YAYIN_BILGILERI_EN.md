### 📝 Video Description

Why do our hands constantly reach for the screen? Why do we crave just one more video, one more swipe, only to be flooded with restlessness, emptiness, and acute anxiety the moment we put the phone away?

In this scientific video essay, Dr. Anna Lembke, Chief of Addiction Medicine at Stanford University and bestselling author of *Dopamine Nation*, unpacks the brain's master biological mechanism: **The Pleasure-Pain Balance.**

Humans evolved over hundreds of thousands of years in a world of harsh scarcity and ever-present danger—programmed to endure physical pain and exhaustion in pursuit of scarce rewards. Today, however, we carry an infinite, friction-free synthetic dopamine dispensary right inside our pockets.

How does the human brain react to this unprecedented sensory flood? Who are the biological "Gremlins" camping out on the pain side of our scale? And why are millions of people no longer consuming digital media to experience joy, but simply to escape baseline agony and feel "normal"?
